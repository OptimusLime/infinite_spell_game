# Storyboard round 1: intent (written before judging; NOT sent to any judge)

Video: the AI game dev "day in the life" for the Infinite Spell Game. Audience: r/aigamedev (solo builders who make
games with AI agents; 36 of the year's top 40 posts there are videos). Cap: 555 spoken words (the v0.6 narration);
aim for ~90-120 s.

## What the video must do (from START_HERE and the brief)
"First, we need something visually interesting, THEN we need item fusion to sit on top of it." The game is made in
one day from Atelico engine parts (the marketplace in use), each part answering a real pain, and fusion is the payoff.

## Takeaways a viewer must reconstruct, in order
1. One person made this game in one working day, in the Atelico editor, with Claude.
2. The game: a cosy pixel town under two moons on different clocks; when they line up, the world turns to frozen
   ink, strange weather falls and monsters rise; you hold the town's seed until the moons part.
3. Most of it was installed, not written: ready-made parts from a marketplace (sky, pixel town, ink look, camera,
   fusion) that work together. Pains answered: code that breaks when you add a mechanic; art that doesn't match;
   agents overwriting each other; per-use AI bills.
4. Everything in the game is a spell (a trigger and an effect), buildings included.
5. Fusion: two spells, or a spell and a building, fuse into a new one, and the order changes the result. This is
   the moment to want.
6. Every part shown is in the marketplace; ask your agent for it.
