1. SPOKEN: "A town under two moons, one purple, one orange. At nine this morning, it didn't exist."
   SHOWN: One continuous move. Start low and cinematic, looking up at the night sky: the purple moon and the orange moon big in the skybox. Pan down without a cut to the game's own camera, the high isometric view over the cosy pixel town, snow on the roofs and lamps lit. Then the character starts walking through the town.
2. SPOKEN: "Nine a.m., in the Atelico editor. I ask Claude for a pixel town under two moons. It doesn't write a renderer: it installs five parts from the Atelico marketplace that are built to fit together, so adding the camera doesn't break the sky."
   SHOWN: A clock in the corner: 9:00. The Atelico editor with one Claude pane. Typed: "a cosy pixel town under two moons, purple and orange". The marketplace panel opens; Claude ticks Pixel world, Moon town, Sky, Ink world and Camera path, and each installs. The scene view starts: the camera cranes down from the moons onto the town.
3. SPOKEN: "In the game, you plant a seed and a town grows around it. If the seed breaks, the town is gone."
   SHOWN: The player presses a glowing seed into open ground; the first houses grow up around it. The seed keeps pulsing at the town's centre with a ring that shows its health.
4. SPOKEN: "The sky part runs each moon on its own clock, so I ask it when they next line up: day 33, just after midnight. I skip ahead."
   SHOWN: Clock: 11:00. In the editor, typed to Claude: "when do the moons line up next?" The forecast panel opens: a ring per moon, a bar per moon's hours in the sky, and "day 33 00:06 · Eclipse night" highlighted. The game's clock spins forward to day 33.
5. SPOKEN: "At midnight the moons meet. The ink-world part restyles every house, tree and townsperson in one pass. Nothing is redrawn, so nothing comes out off-style. The townsfolk freeze."
   SHOWN: The two moons touch in the sky. Hard cut from warm pixels to flat ink: two or three tones, hard black lines, purple and orange the only colours. People stopped mid-step, lamps gone cold.
6. SPOKEN: "Both moons at once: fire falls into the snow."
   SHOWN: A snowfield in the ink world; burning flakes come down and hiss into steam where they land.
7. SPOKEN: "Each moon raises its own creatures. Tonight both are up, so purple ones and orange ones climb out together and head for the seed."
   SHOWN: The ground cracks under both moons' light; purple and orange ink creatures pull themselves up and walk toward the glowing seed.
8. SPOKEN: "Afternoon. The town has to fight back, so I split Claude into three sessions, one per window: town, sky, spells. Each changes only its own files."
   SHOWN: Clock: 14:00. The Claude pane splits into three, labelled Town, Sky, Spells, each beside its own window. Under each, the list of files it is changing; no file appears in two lists.
9. SPOKEN: "The spells agent makes everything a spell: a sensor and an effect. The watchtower is one. Sensor: a creature in my shadow. Effect: slow it."
   SHOWN: The watchtower in the ink night with its spell beside it: sensor "creature in my shadow", effect "slow". Each moon throws its own coloured shadow from the tower; a creature steps into the purple one and drags to a crawl.
10. SPOKEN: "Evening, the last part: fusion. Two spells cast in a row fuse into a new one. Fire then wind: a wall of flame. Wind then fire: a fireball."
   SHOWN: Clock: 18:00. The hero, in the ink world, casts fire then wind (two icons pop above the head in order): a wall of flame rises across the street and stops a line of creatures. Then wind then fire: a fireball arcs into a crowd and bursts.
11. SPOKEN: "The tower is a spell too, so I cast fire into it. Now its effect is slow, then burn. Fusion runs on the player's machine, with no server, so it costs nothing per fusion."
   SHOWN: The hero casts fire at the watchtower; its spell gains a second effect, "burn"; the shadows get burning edges. A creature steps in, slows, and catches fire. A small label: "on-device · no server" (measured on the shoot day).
12. SPOKEN: "Ten p.m., the first full night. The tower holds the street, and the seed holds until the moons part."
   SHOWN: Clock: 22:00. Creatures funnel into the tower's burning shadow; the seed pulses unbroken; above, the moons slide apart.
13. SPOKEN: "Every part in this town, fusion included, is in the Atelico marketplace. Ask your agent for it."
   SHOWN: Dawn: back to warm pixels, the townsfolk moving again. Pull back into the editor: the marketplace with today's six parts highlighted (Pixel world, Moon town, Sky, Ink world, Camera path, Fusion) and the game's name, Infinite Spell Game.
